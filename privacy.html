Privacy Policy - Opportunity Engine

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Opportunity Engine is a personal research tool run by a single independent developer for their own small business. It is not offered to other users and has no public sign-up.

What data the tool accesses

The tool reads aggregated, public trend and market data from official APIs (for example Pinterest trending keywords, Wikipedia pageviews, Bing keyword statistics, YouTube video statistics). It only accesses the developer's own accounts on these services.

Personal data of other people

The tool does not collect, store or process personal data of other people. It does not read other users' profiles, boards, pins, messages or contacts, and it does not post or modify any content.

Storage and sharing

Retrieved data is stored only locally on the developer's own computer and is used only to decide which products to make and sell. It is not sold, shared, published or transferred to third parties. Data is kept only as long as the respective platform's terms allow.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or any tracking.
